Welcome

Hello! I’m Kasieobi Udumaga, a high school student from British Columbia, Canada with a strong interest in pure mathematics.

My primary interests are in algebraic number theory and commutative algebra. I am particularly interested in studying the connections between these areas, including topics such as valuations, factorization, ideals, algebraic number fields, and the structure of rings. I will also explore related areas of mathematics as I develop my background and pursue research questions of my own.

This website documents my mathematical progress through research projects, expository writing, mathematical notes, and explorations of topics ranging from olympiad mathematics to graduate-level mathematics.

Outside of mathematics, I am a sprinter and enjoy track and field. I am a 12-time provincial champion in the 100m and 200m and a co-holder of the electronically timed provincial record for the U15 100m dash.

Major website updates are typically released every 4–6 weeks, though the schedule may vary depending on the time of year.
