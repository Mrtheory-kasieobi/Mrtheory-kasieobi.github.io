Problem Set 6 Solutions: Systems and Inequalities

Precalculus 11 - Solutions
Topics: Systems of Equations, Inequalities

Solutions

Problem 1

Solve by substitution: \(\begin{cases} y = 3x - 2 \\ 2x + y = 8 \end{cases}\)

Solution:

Substitute: \(2x + (3x - 2) = 8\)

\(5x - 2 = 8\)

\(x = 2\)

\(y = 3(2) - 2 = 4\)

Solution: \((2, 4)\)

Problem 2

Solve by elimination: \(\begin{cases} 3x + 4y = 10 \\ 2x - 4y = -2 \end{cases}\)

Solution:

Add equations: \(5x = 8\)

\(x = \frac{8}{5} = 1.6\)

Substitute: \(2(1.6) - 4y = -2\)

\(3.2 - 4y = -2\)

\(y = \frac{2.8}{2} = 0.7\)

Solution: \((1.6, 0.7)\)

Problem 3

The sum of two numbers is 15 and their difference is 3. Find the numbers.

Solution:

Let \(x + y = 15\) and \(x - y = 3\)

Add: \(2x = 18\) → \(x = 9\)

Substitute: \(y = 15 - 9 = 6\)

The numbers are 9 and 6.

Problem 4

A rectangle has a perimeter of 24 cm. If the length is twice the width, find the dimensions.

Solution:

Let \(w\) = width, \(l = 2w\) = length

Perimeter: \(2w + 2(2w) = 24\)

\(6w = 24\)

\(w = 4\) cm, \(l = 8\) cm

Problem 5

Solve: \(4x - 7 > 5\)

Solution:

\(4x > 12\)

\(x > 3\)

Problem 6

Solve: \(-3x + 2 \leq 11\)

Solution:

\(-3x \leq 9\)

\(x \geq -3\) (reverse sign when dividing by negative)

Problem 7

Solve: \(x^2 + 3x - 4 < 0\)

Solution:

Factor: \((x + 4)(x - 1) < 0\)

Roots: \(x = -4\) and \(x = 1\)

Test intervals: The product is negative between the roots

Solution: \(-4 < x < 1\)

Problem 8

Solve: \(2x^2 - 8x + 6 \geq 0\)

Solution:

Factor: \(2(x^2 - 4x + 3) = 2(x - 1)(x - 3) \geq 0\)

Roots: \(x = 1\) and \(x = 3\)

Since the parabola opens upward, the expression is non-negative outside the roots

Solution: \(x \leq 1\) or \(x \geq 3\)

Problem 9

Graph the solution set: \(y \geq x^2 + 2x - 3\)

Solution:

Graph the parabola \(y = x^2 + 2x - 3\) with a solid line

Find vertex: \(x = -1\), \(y = (-1)^2 + 2(-1) - 3 = -2\)

Vertex: \((-1, -2)\)

Test point \((0, 0)\): \(0 \geq 0 + 0 - 3\) → \(0 \geq -3\) ✓

Shade the region above the parabola

Problem 10

Solve the system: \(\begin{cases} y > x - 2 \\ y \leq -x + 4 \end{cases}\)

Solution:

Graph both inequalities and find the overlapping region

The solution is the region between the two lines, bounded by \(y = x - 2\) (dashed) and \(y = -x + 4\) (solid)
