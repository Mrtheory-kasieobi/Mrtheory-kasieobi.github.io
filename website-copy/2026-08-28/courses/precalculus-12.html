Course Description

This course builds on Precalculus 11, exploring advanced functions, transformations, and introducing calculus concepts. Students will study trigonometric equations, rational functions, exponential and logarithmic functions in depth, and begin their journey into limits and derivatives. The course prepares students for university-level mathematics and calculus.
