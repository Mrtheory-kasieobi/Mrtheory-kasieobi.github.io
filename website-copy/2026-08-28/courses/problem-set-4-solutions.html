Problem Set 4 Solutions: Quadratic Equations

Precalculus 11 - Solutions
Topics: Quadratic Equations, Factoring, Quadratic Formula

Solutions

Problem 1

Solve by factoring: \(x^2 - 7x + 12 = 0\)

Solution:

Find two numbers that multiply to 12 and add to -7: -3 and -4

\(x^2 - 7x + 12 = (x - 3)(x - 4) = 0\)

Solutions: \(x = 3\) or \(x = 4\)

Problem 2

Solve by completing the square: \(x^2 + 4x - 5 = 0\)

Solution:

Move constant: \(x^2 + 4x = 5\)

Add \((\frac{4}{2})^2 = 4\): \(x^2 + 4x + 4 = 5 + 4\)

\((x + 2)^2 = 9\)

\(x + 2 = \pm 3\)

Solutions: \(x = 1\) or \(x = -5\)

Problem 3

Solve using the quadratic formula: \(2x^2 - 3x - 4 = 0\)

Solution:

\(a = 2\), \(b = -3\), \(c = -4\)

\(x = \frac{3 \pm \sqrt{9 + 32}}{4} = \frac{3 \pm \sqrt{41}}{4}\)

Solutions: \(x \approx 2.35\) or \(x \approx -1.10\)

Problem 4

Determine the nature of the roots for \(x^2 + 2x + 5 = 0\) without solving.

Solution:

Discriminant: \(D = 4 - 20 = -16\)

Since \(D < 0\), there are no real roots (two complex roots).

Problem 5

Two numbers have a sum of 15 and a product of 50. Find the two numbers.

Solution:

Let the numbers be \(x\) and \(y\). Then \(x + y = 15\) and \(xy = 50\)

From first equation: \(y = 15 - x\)

Substitute: \(x(15 - x) = 50\)

\(15x - x^2 = 50\)

\(x^2 - 15x + 50 = 0\)

\((x - 5)(x - 10) = 0\)

The numbers are 5 and 10.

Problem 6

A rectangle has an area of 48 m² and a length that is 4 m more than its width. Find the dimensions.

Solution:

Let width = \(x\), length = \(x + 4\)

Area: \(x(x + 4) = 48\)

\(x^2 + 4x - 48 = 0\)

\((x + 12)(x - 4) = 0\)

Width = 4 m, Length = 8 m (rejecting negative solution)

Problem 7

Solve: \(3x^2 + 2x - 1 = 0\) using the quadratic formula.

Solution:

\(x = \frac{-2 \pm \sqrt{4 + 12}}{6} = \frac{-2 \pm 4}{6}\)

Solutions: \(x = \frac{1}{3}\) or \(x = -1\)

Problem 8

Find two consecutive integers whose product is 156.

Solution:

Let integers be \(x\) and \(x + 1\)

\(x(x + 1) = 156\)

\(x^2 + x - 156 = 0\)

\(x = \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{1 + 624}}{2} = \frac{-1 \pm 25}{2}\)

\(x = 12\) or \(x = -13\)

The integers are 12 and 13 (or -13 and -12).

Bonus Solutions

Bonus Solution 1

Solution:

If \(x = 1\) is a root, substitute into the equation: \(1^2 - 3(1) + k = 0\)

\(1 - 3 + k = 0\)

\(k = 2\)

With \(k = 2\), the equation is \(x^2 - 3x + 2 = 0\)

Factoring: \((x - 1)(x - 2) = 0\)

The other root is \(x = 2\)

Bonus Solution 2

Solution:

Let roots be \(r\) and \(r+5\). Sum: \(r + (r+5) = 7\), so \(r = 1\).

Roots are 1 and 6. Equation: \((x-1)(x-6) = x^2 - 7x + 6 = 0\)
