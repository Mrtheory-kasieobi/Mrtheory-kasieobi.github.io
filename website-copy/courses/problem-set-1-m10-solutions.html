Problem Set 1 Solutions: Measurement

Mathematics 10 - Chapter 1

Solutions

Solution 1

\(48\) inches \(= 48 \div 12 = 4\) feet

\(4\) feet \(= 4 \times 3 = 12\) inches = \(0.333...\) yards

Solution 2

\(1.75\) m \(= 1.75 \times 100 = 175\) cm

\(175\) cm \(= 175 \times 10 = 1750\) mm

Solution 3

Base area \(B = 6 \times 6 = 36\) m²

Perimeter \(P = 4 \times 6 = 24\) m

\(SA = 36 + \frac{1}{2}(24)(5) = 36 + 60 = 96\) m²

Solution 4

Using \(l^2 = r^2 + h^2\): \(25 = 16 + h^2\), so \(h = 3\) cm

\(V = \frac{1}{3}\pi(4)^2(3) = \frac{1}{3}\pi(48) = 16\pi \approx 50.27\) cm³

Solution 5

\(100\pi = 4\pi r^2\), so \(r^2 = 25\) and \(r = 5\) cm

\(V = \frac{4}{3}\pi(5)^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi(125) = \frac{500\pi}{3} \approx 523.6\) cm³

Solution 6

\(2500\) cm \(= 25\) m \(0\) cm

\(25\) m \(= 2500 \div 30.48 \approx 82.02\) feet

Solution 7

Surface area: \(SA = 2(8 \times 6 + 8 \times 4 + 6 \times 4) = 2(48 + 32 + 24) = 208\) ft²

Volume: \(V = 8 \times 6 \times 4 = 192\) ft³

Solution 8

\(SA = 2\pi(3)^2 + 2\pi(3)(7) = 18\pi + 42\pi = 60\pi \approx 188.5\) m²

\(V = \pi(3)^2(7) = 63\pi \approx 197.9\) m³

Cone volume: \(V_{cone} = \frac{1}{3}\pi(3)^2(3.5) = 10.5\pi \approx 32.99\) m³

Cylinder volume is 6 times the cone volume.

Bonus Problem Solutions

Bonus Solution 1

Step 1: Find the initial volume of water (80% of tank capacity)

Tank volume: \(V_{tank} = \pi(2)^2(5) = 20\pi \approx 62.83\) m³

Initial water volume: \(V_{water} = 0.8 \times 20\pi = 16\pi \approx 50.27\) m³

Step 2: Find the volume of the spherical ball

\(V_{sphere} = \frac{4}{3}\pi(0.5)^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi(0.125) = \frac{0.5\pi}{3} \approx 0.524\) m³

Step 3: Find the new water volume (water + displaced volume)

\(V_{new} = 16\pi + 0.5\pi/3 = \frac{48\pi + 0.5\pi}{3} = \frac{48.5\pi}{3} \approx 50.80\) m³

Step 4: Find the new height using \(V = \pi r^2 h\)

\(h_{new} = \frac{V_{new}}{\pi r^2} = \frac{48.5\pi/3}{\pi(4)} = \frac{48.5}{12} \approx 4.04\) m

Answer: The new water level is approximately \(4.04\) m (an increase of about \(4\) cm).

Bonus Solution 2

Step 1: Use the constraint \(h = 3r\) in the volume formula

\(V = \pi r^2 h = \pi r^2(3r) = 3\pi r^3 = 500\)

Step 2: Solve for \(r\)

\(r^3 = \frac{500}{3\pi} \approx \frac{500}{9.425} \approx 53.05\)

\(r = \sqrt[3]{53.05} \approx 3.76\) cm

Step 3: Find \(h\)

\(h = 3r = 3(3.76) \approx 11.28\) cm

Step 4: Calculate surface area

\(SA = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi rh = 2\pi(3.76)^2 + 2\pi(3.76)(11.28)\)

\(SA = 2\pi(14.14) + 2\pi(42.41) = 28.28\pi + 84.82\pi = 113.1\pi \approx 355.3\) cm²

Answer: Radius \(\approx 3.76\) cm, Height \(\approx 11.28\) cm, Surface Area \(\approx 355.3\) cm²

Bonus Solution 3

Step 1: Find the radius of the cone's base (same base area as pyramid)

Pyramid base area: \(B = 6^2 = 36\) cm²

Cone base area: \(\pi r^2 = 36\), so \(r^2 = \frac{36}{\pi} \approx 11.46\)

\(r = \sqrt{11.46} \approx 3.38\) cm

Step 2: Calculate pyramid surface area

\(SA_{pyramid} = B + \frac{1}{2}Pl = 36 + \frac{1}{2}(24)(5) = 36 + 60 = 96\) cm²

Step 3: Calculate cone surface area

\(SA_{cone} = \pi r^2 + \pi rl = 36 + \pi(3.38)(5) = 36 + 17.9\pi \approx 36 + 56.3 = 92.3\) cm²

Step 4: Compare

Difference: \(96 - 92.3 = 3.7\) cm²

Answer: The cone's base radius is approximately \(3.38\) cm. The pyramid has the larger surface area by approximately \(3.7\) cm².
