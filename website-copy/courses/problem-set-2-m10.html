Problem Set 2: Trigonometry

Mathematics 10 - Chapter 2 Medium

Problems

Problem 1

A ladder leans against a wall, reaching \(8\) m up the wall. If the angle with the ground is \(60°\) and the ladder is \(10\) m long, find the distance from the base of the wall to the ladder's foot using \(\sin 60° = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\) and \(\cos 60° = \frac{1}{2}\).

Problem 2

From \(30\) m away, the angle of elevation to the top of a tree is \(25°\). How tall is the tree? Use \(\tan 25° \approx 0.4663\).

Problem 3

A right triangle has legs of length \(5\) cm and \(12\) cm. Find the sine, cosine, and tangent of the angle opposite the \(5\) cm side.

Problem 4

A surveyor measures the angle to the top of a building as \(35°\) from \(20\) m away. Find the building's height. Then find the angle if the surveyor moves \(5\) m closer.

Problem 5

Two observers \(40\) m apart both look at the same airplane. The angles of elevation are \(30°\) and \(45°\). Find the airplane's altitude using the tangent ratio.

Problem 6

A right triangle has hypotenuse \(10\) and one angle \(40°\). Find the other two sides using \(\sin 40° \approx 0.6428\) and \(\cos 40° \approx 0.7660\).

Problem 7

A kite flies at a height of \(40\) m. If the string makes a \(65°\) angle with the ground, how long is the string? Use \(\sin 65° \approx 0.9063\).

Problem 8

Find the angle of elevation to the top of a \(15\) m tall building from \(20\) m away. Use \(\tan \theta = \frac{15}{20} = 0.75\) and \(\tan^{-1}(0.75) \approx 36.87°\).

Bonus Problems

Bonus Problem 1

Challenge: Two buildings stand on level ground. From the top of the shorter building (\(20\) m tall), the angle of elevation to the top of the taller building is \(35°\) and the angle of depression to the base of the taller building is \(40°\). The buildings are \(30\) m apart. Find the height of the taller building. (Hint: Use both angles to create two right triangles sharing a common horizontal distance.)

Bonus Problem 2

Challenge: A hot air balloon is ascending vertically at a constant speed. From a point on the ground \(100\) m from the launch site, the angle of elevation to the balloon is \(25°\) after \(2\) minutes. After another \(3\) minutes, the angle of elevation is \(60°\). Find the constant ascent speed of the balloon in meters per minute. (Hint: Use tangent ratios at both time points and the horizontal distance remains \(100\) m.)

Bonus Problem 3

Challenge: A lighthouse is located on a cliff \(50\) m above sea level. From a ship at sea, the angle of elevation to the top of the lighthouse is \(30°\) and the angle of elevation to the base of the lighthouse (top of the cliff) is \(15°\). Find the height of the lighthouse itself. Use \(\tan 30° \approx 0.5774\) and \(\tan 15° \approx 0.2679\).
