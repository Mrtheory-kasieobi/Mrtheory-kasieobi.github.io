Problem Set 2 Solutions: Trigonometry

Mathematics 10 - Chapter 2

Solutions

Solution 1

Using \(\cos 60° = \frac{1}{2}\): distance \(= 10 \times \frac{1}{2} = 5\) m

Using \(\sin 60° = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\): height \(= 10 \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 5\sqrt{3} \approx 8.66\) m

Solution 2

Height \(= 30 \times \tan 25° = 30 \times 0.4663 \approx 13.99\) m

Solution 3

Hypotenuse \(= \sqrt{5^2 + 12^2} = 13\) cm

\(\sin \theta = \frac{5}{13}\), \(\cos \theta = \frac{12}{13}\), \(\tan \theta = \frac{5}{12}\)

Solution 4

Height \(= 20 \times \tan 35° \approx 14.28\) m

From \(15\) m away: \(\tan \theta = \frac{14.28}{15}\), so \(\theta \approx 43.2°\)

Solution 5

From \(40\) m: altitude \(= 40 \times \tan 30° = 40 \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \approx 34.64\) m

From \(0\) m: altitude \(= 40 \times \tan 45° = 40\) m

Using elimination: altitude \(= \frac{40(\tan 45° - \tan 30°)}{\tan 45° - \tan 30°} = 40\) m (approximately)

Solution 6

Side 1: \(10 \times \sin 40° \approx 10 \times 0.6428 = 6.428\) cm

Side 2: \(10 \times \cos 40° \approx 10 \times 0.7660 = 7.660\) cm

Solution 7

String length \(= \frac{40}{\sin 65°} = \frac{40}{0.9063} \approx 44.13\) m

Solution 8

\(\theta = \tan^{-1}(0.75) \approx 36.87°\)

Bonus Problem Solutions

Bonus Solution 1

Step 1: Find the height difference between the buildings using the angle of depression

From the top of the shorter building, the angle of depression to the base of the taller building is \(40°\).

Height difference \(= 30 \times \tan 40° \approx 30 \times 0.8391 \approx 25.17\) m

Step 2: Find the additional height using the angle of elevation

Additional height \(= 30 \times \tan 35° \approx 30 \times 0.7002 \approx 21.01\) m

Step 3: Find the total height of the taller building

Total height \(= 20 + 25.17 + 21.01 \approx 66.18\) m

Answer: The taller building is approximately \(66.2\) m tall.

Bonus Solution 2

Step 1: Set up equations for the balloon height at both time points

After 2 minutes: \(h_1 = 100 \times \tan 25° \approx 100 \times 0.4663 \approx 46.63\) m

After 5 minutes (2 + 3): \(h_2 = 100 \times \tan 60° \approx 100 \times 1.732 \approx 173.2\) m

Step 2: Find the height gained in 3 minutes

Height gained \(= 173.2 - 46.63 \approx 126.57\) m

Step 3: Calculate the ascent speed

Speed \(= \frac{126.57}{3} \approx 42.19\) m/min

Answer: The balloon ascends at approximately \(42.2\) meters per minute.

Bonus Solution 3

Step 1: Find the horizontal distance from the ship to the cliff

Using the angle to the base of the lighthouse: \(\tan 15° = \frac{50}{d}\)

\(d = \frac{50}{\tan 15°} \approx \frac{50}{0.2679} \approx 186.6\) m

Step 2: Find the total height from the ship to the top of the lighthouse

\(\tan 30° = \frac{50 + h}{186.6}\)

\(50 + h = 186.6 \times \tan 30° \approx 186.6 \times 0.5774 \approx 107.7\) m

Step 3: Find the height of the lighthouse

\(h = 107.7 - 50 \approx 57.7\) m

Answer: The lighthouse is approximately \(57.7\) m tall.
