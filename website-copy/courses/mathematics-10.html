Level: High School Status: In Progress Last Updated: 2026

Course Information

  • Level High School
  • Estimated Duration 12 weeks
  • Prerequisites Mathematics 9
  • Recommended Textbooks Foundations of Mathematics and Precalculus 10 (Pearson)
  • Software Desmos, GeoGebra

Course Description

This course builds on Mathematics 9 concepts and prepares students for Precalculus 11. Students will explore linear relations, polynomials, systems of equations, coordinate geometry, and introductory trigonometry. The course emphasizes problem-solving, algebraic manipulation, and the connections between different mathematical representations.

Course Syllabus

  1. Week 1 Measurement
  2. Week 2 Trigonometry
  3. Week 3 Factors and Products
  4. Week 4 Roots and Powers
  5. Week 5 Relations and Functions
  6. Week 6 Linear Functions
  7. Week 7 Systems of Linear Equations
  8. Week 8 Review and Applications
  9. Week 9 Final Review and Exam Preparation
