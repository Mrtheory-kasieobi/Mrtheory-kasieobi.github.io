Frequently Asked Questions

+ Can I report mistakes?

Absolutely - and thank you in advance! Mathematics is a collaborative discipline, and I welcome corrections of any kind, whether they are typographical errors, conceptual mistakes, or suggestions for clearer exposition. Please send me an email with the details and I'll make sure to address them promptly.

+ Can I ask mathematics questions?

Of course! I enjoy discussing mathematics at all levels. Whether you're a fellow student working through a problem, someone curious about a concept you've encountered, or a researcher exploring ideas - feel free to reach out. I'll do my best to help or point you toward useful resources.

+ Can I use your notes?

Yes, my notes are freely available for personal study and educational use. If you're an instructor and would like to adapt them for your course, please reach out - I'm generally happy to grant permission. In all cases, I ask that you provide appropriate attribution and respect the spirit of open knowledge sharing.
