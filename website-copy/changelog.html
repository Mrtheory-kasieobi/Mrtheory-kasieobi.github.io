A Rank-31 Elliptic Curve: A New Record in the Search for High-Rank Curves

August 23, 2026

Curve #302, found by Claude, Levent Alpöge, and Ava Howell, establishes rank at least 31 with 31 independent rational points. Under BSD + GRH, the rank is exactly 31. Includes the complete Weierstrass equation, witness points, and analysis of the historical rank progression.
