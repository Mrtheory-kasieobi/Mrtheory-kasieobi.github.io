Course Description

This course continues the study of calculus, focusing on integration techniques, sequences and series, and an introduction to multivariable calculus. Students will master various integration methods, explore convergence of series, and learn to apply calculus to functions of several variables. The course develops problem-solving skills and mathematical maturity.

Course Syllabus

  1. Week 1 Integration by Parts
  2. Week 2 Trigonometric Integrals
  3. Week 3 Trigonometric Substitution
  4. Week 4 Partial Fractions
  5. Week 5 Improper Integrals
  6. Week 6 Sequences and Series
  7. Week 7 Convergence Tests
  8. Week 8 Power Series and Taylor Series
  9. Week 9 Parametric Equations and Polar Coordinates
  10. Week 10 Introduction to Multivariable Calculus
  11. Week 11 Partial Derivatives
  12. Week 12 Multiple Integrals
  13. Week 13 Review and Midterm
  14. Week 14 Final Review and Exam Preparation
