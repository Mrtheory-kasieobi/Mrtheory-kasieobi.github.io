Problem Set 7: Exponential and Logarithmic Functions

Precalculus 12 - Problem Set
Topics: Exponential Growth/Decay, Logarithms, Applications Medium

Problems

Problem 1

Solve: \(2^{x+3} = 8^{x-1}\). Express your answer in terms of logarithms.

Problem 2

Simplify: \(\log_2(8) + \log_2(x) - \log_2(4) + \log_2(x-2)\)

Problem 3

A population grows according to \(P(t) = 1000e^{0.03t}\). When will it reach 2000? What is the doubling time?

Problem 4

Solve: \(\ln(x + 5) - \ln(2) = 3\). Check your answer.

Problem 5

Carbon-14 has half-life 5730 years. If 25% remains, how old is the sample? Use \(A = A_0e^{-kt}\) with \(k = \frac{\ln 2}{5730}\).

Problem 6

Graph \(f(x) = 2^x - 3\) and \(g(x) = \log_2(x + 1)\) on the same axes. Identify domain, range, and asymptotes.

Problem 7

Find the inverse of \(f(x) = 3e^{2x-1} + 5\).

Problem 8

Newton's Law of Cooling: \(T(t) = T_s + (T_0 - T_s)e^{-kt}\). A coffee at 95°C cools in a 20°C room. After 10 min it's 75°C. Find k and when it reaches 30°C.

Bonus Problems

Bonus Problem 1

Find all real solutions to: \(\log_3(x + 1) + \log_3(x - 1) = 2\).

Bonus Problem 2

Use the change of base formula to express \(\log_5(7)\) in terms of natural logarithms. Then solve for \(x\) if \(\log_5(7) = \frac{\ln x}{\ln 3}\).
