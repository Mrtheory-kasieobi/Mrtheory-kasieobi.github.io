Problem Set 12: Midterm Review

Calculus I - Week 11

Instructions

Show all work. No calculators. Time limit: 90 minutes.

Problems

  • 1. Find the domain of \(f(x) = \frac{\sqrt{x^2 - 4}}{x - 1}\)
  • 2. Evaluate \(\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^x - 1 - x}{x^2}\)
  • 3. Use the definition to find \(f'(x)\) for \(f(x) = \frac{1}{x}\)
  • 4. Find \(\frac{d}{dx}[x^{\sin x}]\) using logarithmic differentiation
  • 5. Find the equation of the tangent line to \(y = \ln(\sin x)\) at \(x = \frac{\pi}{4}\)
  • 6. Use implicit differentiation to find \(\frac{dy}{dx}\) for \(x^3 + y^3 = 3xy\) (folium of Descartes)
  • 7. A spherical balloon is inflated at 10 cm³/s. How fast is the radius increasing when diameter is 20 cm?
  • 8. Find the dimensions of the rectangle with perimeter 20 having maximum area
  • 9. Verify the MVT for \(f(x) = x^3 - 3x^2 + 2\) on \([0, 2]\)
  • 10. Sketch \(f(x) = \frac{x^2 - 1}{x^2 + 1}\) by finding critical points, inflection points, and asymptotes
  • 11. Evaluate \(\int_0^1 x e^x \, dx\)
  • 12. Find \(\int \frac{\ln x}{x} \, dx\)
Bonus Problems (10 points each):
  • B1. Find the area between \(y = \sin x\) and \(y = \cos x\) on \([0, \frac{\pi}{4}]\)
  • B2. Prove that if \(f\) is differentiable and \(f'(x) > 0\) for all \(x\), then \(f\) is strictly increasing
