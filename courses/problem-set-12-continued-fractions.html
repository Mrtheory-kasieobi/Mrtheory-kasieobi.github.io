Problem Set 12: Continued Fractions

Elementary Number Theory - Week 13 Medium

Problems

Problem 1

Find the continued fraction expansion of \(\frac{19}{7}\) and compute its convergents.

Problem 2

Compute the continued fraction expansion of \(\sqrt{7}\) and determine its period length.

Problem 3

Find the fundamental solution to \(x^2 - 2y^2 = 1\) using continued fractions.

Problem 4

Find the continued fraction expansion of \(\sqrt{13}\) and its period length.

Problem 5

Find the fundamental solution to \(x^2 - 13y^2 = 1\).

Problem 6

Show that every rational number has a finite continued fraction expansion.

Problem 7

Prove: if \(\frac{p}{q}\) is a convergent to \(\alpha\), then \(|q\alpha - p| < 1\).

Problem 8

Find all solutions to \(x^2 - 3y^2 = 1\) with \(x, y < 100\).

Problem 9

Prove that consecutive convergents satisfy \(|p_n q_{n+1} - p_{n+1} q_n| = 1\).

Problem 10

Show that the convergents of an irrational number are always in lowest terms.

Bonus Problems

Bonus Problem 1

Challenge: Find the continued fraction expansion of \(\frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}\) and verify it is \([1; \overline{1}]\).

Bonus Problem 2

Challenge: Compute the first 5 convergents of \(\sqrt{3}\) using the recurrence relations.

Bonus Problem 3

Challenge: Prove that if \(\frac{p_n}{q_n}\) and \(\frac{p_{n+1}}{q_{n+1}}\) are consecutive convergents, then \(\left|\alpha - \frac{p_n}{q_n}\right| < \frac{1}{q_n q_{n+1}}\).
