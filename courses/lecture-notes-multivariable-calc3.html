Multivariable Calculus

Calculus III - Chapter 12-14

1. Three-Dimensional Coordinate Systems

3D Coordinates: Points in \(\mathbb{R}^3\) are represented as \((x, y, z)\). Surfaces are defined by equations \(F(x, y, z) = 0\).

2. Vectors

Vector Operations:
  • Addition: \(\vec{a} + \vec{b} = (a_1 + b_1, a_2 + b_2, a_3 + b_3)\)
  • Scalar multiplication: \(c\vec{a} = (ca_1, ca_2, ca_3)\)
  • Dot product: \(\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = a_1b_1 + a_2b_2 + a_3b_3\)
  • Cross product: \(\vec{a} \times \vec{b} = (a_2b_3 - a_3b_2, a_3b_1 - a_1b_3, a_1b_2 - a_2b_1)\)

3. Partial Derivatives

Partial Derivative: \(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x} = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h, y) - f(x, y)}{h}\)
Gradient Vector: \(\nabla f = \left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}, \frac{\partial f}{\partial z}\right)\)

4. Multiple Integrals

Double Integral: \(\iint_R f(x, y) \, dA = \int_a^b \int_{g_1(x)}^{g_2(x)} f(x, y) \, dy \, dx\)
Coordinate Systems:
  • Polar: \(dA = r \, dr \, d\theta\)
  • Cylindrical: \(dV = r \, dr \, d\theta \, dz\)
  • Spherical: \(dV = \rho^2 \sin\phi \, d\rho \, d\phi \, d\theta\)

5. Vector Fields and Line Integrals

Line Integral: \(\int_C \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r} = \int_a^b \vec{F}(\vec{r}(t)) \cdot \vec{r}'(t) \, dt\)
Fundamental Theorem for Line Integrals: If \(\vec{F} = \nabla f\), then \(\int_C \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r} = f(\vec{r}(b)) - f(\vec{r}(a))\)

6. Green's, Stokes', and Divergence Theorems

Green's Theorem: \(\oint_C \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r} = \iint_D \left(\frac{\partial Q}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial P}{\partial y}\right) dA\)
Stokes' Theorem: \(\oint_C \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r} = \iint_S (\nabla \times \vec{F}) \cdot \vec{n} \, dS\)
Divergence Theorem: \(\iiint_E (\nabla \cdot \vec{F}) \, dV = \oiint_S \vec{F} \cdot \vec{n} \, dS\)
