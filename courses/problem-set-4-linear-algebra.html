Problem Set 4: Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors

Linear Algebra Course
Based on Axler's "Linear Algebra Done Right"

Instructions

Solve all problems. Show your work clearly. You may use results from previous lectures.

Problem 4.1

Find all eigenvalues and eigenvectors of \(A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}\).

Problem 4.2

Determine if \(A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}\) is diagonalizable. Justify your answer.

Problem 4.3

Find an invertible matrix \(P\) such that \(P^{-1} \begin{pmatrix} 4 & -2 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} P\) is diagonal.

Problem 4.4

Let \(A\) be an \(n \times n\) matrix with eigenvalues \(\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \ldots, \lambda_n\) (not necessarily distinct). Find the eigenvalues of \(A^2\) and \(A^{-1}\) (if invertible).

Problem 4.5

Prove that if \(v\) is an eigenvector of \(A\) with eigenvalue \(\lambda\), then \(v\) is also an eigenvector of \(A^k\) for any positive integer \(k\).
