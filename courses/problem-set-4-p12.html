Problem Set 4: Trigonometry and Unit Circle

Precalculus 12 - Problem Set
Topics: Angles, Unit Circle, Trigonometric Ratios Medium

Problems

Problem 1

Convert \(120°\) to radians. Find the reference angle and determine the sign of sine, cosine, and tangent in this quadrant.

Problem 2

Find all six trigonometric values for \(\theta = \frac{5\pi}{6}\). Explain the sign of each in quadrant II.

Problem 3

Solve \(\sin\theta = \frac{1}{2}\) for \(\theta \in [0, 2\pi)\) and give the general solution.

Problem 4

A lighthouse is 2 km from point P on shore. The light rotates at 5 rev/min. Find the speed of the beam along the shore when it is 1 km from P.

Problem 5

Find the arc length and sector area of a circle with radius 5 cm and central angle \(2.5\) radians.

Problem 6

If \(\cos\theta = -\frac{4}{5}\) and \(\theta\) is in quadrant III, find \(\sin\theta\) and \(\tan\theta\).

Problem 7

Find the exact value of \(\sin(105°)\) using sum identities.

Problem 8

A wheel with radius 10 cm rotates through an angle of \(75°\). Find the arc length traveled by a point on the rim.

Bonus Problems

Bonus Problem 1

Find all solutions to \(\sin(2\theta) = \cos\theta\) in the interval \([0, 2\pi)\).

Bonus Problem 2

A ladder leaning against a wall makes a \(60°\) angle with the ground. If the foot of the ladder is 2 ft from the wall, how far up the wall does the ladder reach? What angle would give the same height with one-third the distance from the wall?
