Course Description

This course provides a rigorous introduction to real analysis, developing the theoretical foundations of calculus. Students will explore the real number system, sequences and series, continuity, differentiation, and integration from a proof-based perspective. The course emphasizes mathematical rigor, logical reasoning, and the epsilon-delta framework that underlies modern analysis.

Course Syllabus

  1. Week 1 The Real Number System
  2. Week 2 Sequences and Limits
  3. Week 3 Cauchy Sequences and Completeness
  4. Week 4 Series and Convergence Tests
  5. Week 5 Continuity and the Intermediate Value Theorem
  6. Week 6 Differentiation and the Mean Value Theorem
  7. Week 7 Taylor's Theorem and Approximations
  8. Week 8 Riemann Integration

  10. Week 10 Sequences and Series of Functions

  13. Week 13 Review and Midterm
  14. Week 14 Final Review and Exam Preparation
