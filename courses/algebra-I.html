Course Description

This course provides a rigorous introduction to abstract algebra, following the structure of MIT's 18.701 Algebra I. Topics include group theory, linear algebra, and geometry with emphasis on proofs and theoretical understanding. The course is more extensive and theoretical than standard algebra sequences, requiring experience with mathematical proofs.

Course Syllabus

  1. Week 1 Group Theory: Basic Concepts
  2. Week 2 Subgroups and Cyclic Groups
  3. Week 3 Cosets and Lagrange's Theorem
  4. Week 4 Normal Subgroups and Quotient Groups
  5. Week 5 Isomorphism Theorems
  6. Week 6 Group Actions and Sylow Theorems
  7. Week 7 Rings and Fields: Definitions
  8. Week 8 Polynomial Rings and Factorization
  9. Week 9 Euclidean Domains and PID
  10. Week 10 Unique Factorization Domains
  11. Week 11 Review and Applications
  12. Week 12 Final Review and Exam Preparation
