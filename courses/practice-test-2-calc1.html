Worked Solutions

Each solution gives one route and, where useful, an alternative. If your work used a different valid method, you should still receive credit; the grader accepts common equivalent forms.

  1. 1. This is the derivative of \(e^x\) at \(0\): \(\lim_{x\to0}\frac{e^x-1}{x}=1\). Alternative: use \(e^x=1+x+\frac{x^2}{2}+\cdots\), so the quotient tends to \(1\).
  2. 2. Product rule: \((x^3\ln x)'=3x^2\ln x+x^3\cdot\frac1x=3x^2\ln x+x^2=x^2(3\ln x+1)\).
  3. 3. \(f'(x)=3x^2-12x+9=3(x-1)(x-3)\), so the critical points are \(x=1,3\). Alternative: solve \(x^2-4x+3=0\) by factoring.
  4. 4. Since \(\frac{d}{dx}\arcsin x=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\), the integral is \(\arcsin x+C\). Alternative: \(-\arccos x+C\) differs only by a constant.
  5. 5. Washers: \(V=\pi\int_0^1(x^2)^2\,dx=\pi\left[\frac{x^5}{5}\right]_0^1=\frac{\pi}{5}\).
  6. 6. \(\frac{d}{dx}\tan^{-1}x=\frac{1}{1+x^2}\). Alternative: if \(y=\tan^{-1}x\), then \(\tan y=x\), so \(\sec^2y\,y'=1\) and \(y'=\cos^2y=\frac{1}{1+\tan^2y}=\frac{1}{1+x^2}\).
  7. 7. The logarithm requires \(x^2-4>0\), i.e. \(|x|>2\), so the domain is \((-\infty,-2)\cup(2,\infty)\).
  8. 8. \(\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos x\,dx=[\sin x]_0^{\pi/2}=1-0=1\).
  9. 9. \(y-5=3(x-2)\), hence \(y=3x-1\).
  10. 10. Average value \(=\int_0^1 e^x\,dx=[e^x]_0^1=e-1\).
  11. 11. \(\int(x+3)\,dx=\frac{x^2}{2}+3x+C\). Differentiate to verify.
  12. 12. At \(x=1\), \(y=0\) and \(y'=4x-3=1\). Thus \(y-0=1(x-1)\), i.e. \(y=x-1\).
  13. 13. Let \(u=x^2+1\), \(du=2x\,dx\). Then \(\int\frac{2x}{x^2+1}\,dx=\ln(x^2+1)+C\) since \(x^2+1>0\).
  14. 14. Integrate by parts with \(u=x\), \(dv=\cos x\,dx\): \(\int_0^{\pi/2}x\cos x\,dx=[x\sin x+\cos x]_0^{\pi/2}=\frac{\pi}{2}-1\).
  15. 15. Rewrite as \((x^2+1)^{1/2}\) and use the chain rule: \(\frac12(x^2+1)^{-1/2}(2x)=\frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+1}}\).
  16. 16. On \([0,1]\), \(x\ge x^2\), so \(A=\int_0^1(x-x^2)\,dx=\frac12-\frac13=\frac16\). Alternative (horizontal slices): \(\int_0^1(\sqrt y-y)\,dy=\frac16\).
  17. 17. \(\int_0^1(1-x^3)\,dx=\left[x-\frac{x^4}{4}\right]_0^1=1-\frac14=\frac34\).
  18. 18. Displacement \(=\int_0^2(2-t^2)\,dt=\left[2t-\frac{t^3}{3}\right]_0^2=4-\frac83=\frac43\).
  19. 19. Washers: \(V=\pi\int_0^4(\sqrt x)^2\,dx=\pi\int_0^4x\,dx=\pi\left[\frac{x^2}{2}\right]_0^4=8\pi\). Alternative (shells): \(2\pi\int_0^2 y(4-y^2)\,dy=8\pi\).
  20. 20. \(\int(x+\sin x)\,dx=\frac{x^2}{2}-\cos x+C\). The derivative of \(-\cos x\) is \(\sin x\).
