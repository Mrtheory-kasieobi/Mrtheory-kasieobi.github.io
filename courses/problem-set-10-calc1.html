Problem Set 10: Applications of Integration

Calculus I - Week 9

Instructions

Show all work. No calculators. Write clearly and show each step.

Problems

  • 1. Find the area between \(y = x^3\) and \(y = x\) on \([-1, 1]\)
  • 2. Find the volume of the solid obtained by rotating the region bounded by \(y = x^2\), \(y = 0\), \(x = 2\) about the x-axis
  • 3. Find the volume using the shell method: region bounded by \(y = x^2\), \(y = 0\), \(x = 2\) rotated about the y-axis
  • 4. Find the volume of the solid obtained by rotating the region bounded by \(y = \sin x\), \(y = 0\), \(x = 0\), \(x = \pi\) about the x-axis
  • 5. A tank has the shape of an inverted cone with height 10 m and base radius 4 m. If water is being pumped in at 2 m³/min, how fast is the water level rising when the water is 5 m deep?
  • 6. Find the work required to pump all the water out of a cylindrical tank (radius 3 m, height 5 m) full of water. Water density = 1000 kg/m³, \(g = 9.8\) m/s²
  • 7. Find the center of mass of the region bounded by \(y = x^2\) and \(y = x\)
  • 8. Find the average temperature over the interval \([0, \pi]\) if \(T(t) = 50 + 20\cos t\) degrees Fahrenheit
  • 9. Find the arc length of \(y = x^{3/2}\) from \(x = 0\) to \(x = 4\) (bonus: the formula is \(\int_a^b \sqrt{1 + (f'(x))^2} \, dx\))
  • 10. A spring exerts a force of 10 N when stretched 0.5 m. Find the work done in stretching the spring from 0.5 m to 1 m
Bonus Problems (10 points each):
  • B1. Find the volume of the solid obtained by rotating the region bounded by \(xy = 1\), \(y = 0\), \(x = 1\), \(x = 2\) about the y-axis
  • B2. Prove that the work formula \(W = \int_a^b F(x) \, dx\) gives the correct units (force × distance)
