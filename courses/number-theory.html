Elementary Number Theory

Divisibility, primes, congruences, and classical number-theoretic problems.

Course Description

This course provides an introduction to elementary number theory, exploring the properties of integers and the beautiful patterns that emerge from their study. Topics include divisibility, prime numbers, congruences, Diophantine equations, and quadratic residues. The course balances computational techniques with theoretical understanding, laying the foundation for algebraic number theory.

Course Syllabus

  1. Week 1 Introduction to Number Theory
  2. Week 2 Divisibility and the Division Algorithm
  3. Week 3 Prime Numbers and the Fundamental Theorem
  4. Week 4 Greatest Common Divisors and Euclidean Algorithm
  5. Week 5 Modular Arithmetic and Congruences
  6. Week 6 Solving Linear Congruences
  7. Week 7 Chinese Remainder Theorem
  8. Week 8 Prime Numbers and Factorization
  9. Week 9 Fermat's Little Theorem and Euler's Theorem
  10. Week 10 Primitive Roots and Discrete Logarithms
  11. Week 11 Quadratic Residues and Reciprocity
  12. Week 12 Diophantine Equations
  13. Week 13 Review and Applications
  14. Week 14 Final Review and Exam Preparation
