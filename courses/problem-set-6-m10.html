Problem Set 6: Linear Functions

Mathematics 10 - Chapter 6 Medium

Problems

Problem 1

Find the slope and y-intercept of \(y = 3x - 7\)

Problem 2

Find the equation of a line passing through \((2, 5)\) and \((6, 13)\)

Problem 3

Graph \(2x + 3y \leq 6\) and shade the correct region

Problem 4

Solve: \(4(x - 3) + 2 = 3x + 5\)

Problem 5

Find \(f(2)\), \(f(-1)\), and \(f(0)\) for \(f(x) = -2x + 5\)

Problem 6

Write in point-slope form: a line with slope \(4\) passing through \((-1, 3)\)

Problem 7

Find the x-intercept and y-intercept of \(3x - 4y = 12\)

Problem 8

Two lines have slopes \(m_1 = 2\) and \(m_2 = -3\). Are they parallel, perpendicular, or neither?

Bonus Problems

Bonus Problem 1 Medium

Slope Detective: A line passes through two lattice points (points with integer coordinates). Prove that the slope of such a line must be a rational number. Then find a line passing through two lattice points that has a slope of \(\frac{3}{4}\) and has a y-intercept that is also a rational number.

Bonus Problem 2 Hard

Intersection Puzzle: Two lines are given: Line A has equation \(y = 2x + 3\) and Line B has equation \(y = -x + 9\). Find their intersection point, then determine the perpendicular distance from the origin (0, 0) to each line. Which line is closer to the origin?

Bonus Problem 3 Hard

System Challenge: Solve the system: \(y = 2x + 1\) and \(y^2 = 4x + 1\). Show that the system has exactly one solution, and find that solution algebraically. Verify by substitution.
