Problem Set 9: Gaussian Primes and Factorization

Elementary Number Theory - Week 10 Medium

Problems

Problem 1

Find all Gaussian primes with norm less than 10.

Problem 2

Determine which rational primes remain prime in \(\mathbb{Z}[i]\).

Problem 3

Factor 5 in \(\mathbb{Z}[i]\).

Problem 4

Factor 7 in \(\mathbb{Z}[i]\).

Problem 5

Show that if \(p \equiv 3 \pmod{4}\) is prime, then \(p\) remains prime in \(\mathbb{Z}[i]\).

Problem 6

Factor 13 in \(\mathbb{Z}[i]\).

Problem 7

Prove: If \(p \equiv 1 \pmod{4}\) is prime, then \(p\) splits in \(\mathbb{Z}[i]\).

Problem 8

Find the prime factorization of 65 in \(\mathbb{Z}[i]\).

Problem 9

Factor 50 in \(\mathbb{Z}[i]\).

Problem 10

Show that the factorization of primes in \(\mathbb{Z}[i]\) is unique up to units.

Bonus Problems

Bonus Problem 1

Challenge: Prove that if \(p \equiv 1 \pmod{4}\), then \(p = a^2 + b^2\) for some integers \(a, b\).

Bonus Problem 2

Challenge: Show that every prime \(p \equiv 1 \pmod{4}\) can be written as a sum of two squares in essentially one way.

Bonus Problem 3

Challenge: Prove that \(\mathbb{Z}[i]\) is a unique factorization domain.
