British Columbia Precalculus 11

A comprehensive introduction to algebra, functions, and trigonometry for high school students.

Level: High School Status: Complete Last Updated: 2026

Course Information

  • Level High School
  • Estimated Duration 12 weeks
  • Prerequisites Mathematics 10
  • Recommended Textbooks McGraw-Hill Pre-Calculus 11
  • Software Desmos, GeoGebra

Course Description

This course covers the foundational topics in algebra, functions, and trigonometry as outlined in the British Columbia Precalculus 11 curriculum. Students will develop a deep understanding of polynomial, rational, and trigonometric functions, along with their applications. The course emphasizes problem-solving, mathematical reasoning, and the connections between algebraic and graphical representations.

Course Syllabus

  1. Week 1 Sequences and Series
  2. Week 2 Sequences and Series (continued)
  3. Week 3 Trigonometry: Angles and the Unit Circle
  4. Week 4 Radical Expressions and Equations
  5. Week 5 Rational Expressions and Equations
  6. Week 6 Absolute Value and Reciprocal Functions
  7. Week 7 Systems of Equations and Inequalities
  8. Week 8 Review and Applications
  9. Week 9 Review and Exam Preparation
