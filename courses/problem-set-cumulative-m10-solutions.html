Problem 1

Convert \(5.2 \text{ m}\) to centimeters and millimeters.

Solution:

For centimeters: \(5.2 \text{ m} \times 100 \text{ cm/m} = 520 \text{ cm}\)

For millimeters: \(5.2 \text{ m} \times 1000 \text{ mm/m} = 5200 \text{ mm}\)

Using dimensional analysis helps ensure units cancel correctly.

Problem 2

A rectangular prism has dimensions \(8 \text{ cm} \times 5 \text{ cm} \times 3 \text{ cm}\).

Solution:

Surface area: \(SA = 2(lw + lh + wh) = 2(8\times5 + 8\times3 + 5\times3) = 2(40 + 24 + 15) = 158 \text{ cm}^2\)

Volume: \(V = l \times w \times h = 8 \times 5 \times 3 = 120 \text{ cm}^3\)

When dimensions double: \(SA = 632 \text{ cm}^2\) (4× original), \(V = 960 \text{ cm}^3\) (8× original)

Problem 3

A cylindrical water tank has radius \(r = 2 \text{ m}\) and height \(h = 5 \text{ m}\).

Solution:

Surface area: \(SA = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi rh = 2\pi(2)^2 + 2\pi(2)(5) = 8\pi + 20\pi = 28\pi \approx 87.96 \text{ m}^2\)

Volume: \(V = \pi r^2 h = \pi(2)^2(5) = 20\pi \approx 62.83 \text{ m}^3\)

Time to fill: \(t = \frac{62.83 \text{ m}^3}{0.5 \text{ m}^3/\text{min}} \approx 125.7 \text{ min} \approx 2 \text{ hr } 6 \text{ min}\)

Problem 4

In a right triangle, the hypotenuse is \(15 \text{ cm}\) and one angle is \(35°\).

Solution:

Opposite to \(35°\): \(15 \times \sin(35°) \approx 15 \times 0.574 \approx 8.61 \text{ cm}\)

Adjacent to \(35°\): \(15 \times \cos(35°) \approx 15 \times 0.819 \approx 12.29 \text{ cm}\)

Problem 5

A ladder leans against a wall, making a \(75°\) angle with the ground. If the foot of the ladder is \(3 \text{ feet}\) from the wall,

Solution:

\(\cos(75°) = \frac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}} = \frac{3}{\text{leg}}\)

\(\text{leg} = \frac{3}{\cos(75°)} \approx \frac{3}{0.259} \approx 11.58 \text{ feet}\)

Problem 6

Factor completely: \(6x^3 - 12x^2 - 144x\)

Solution:

Factor out GCF: \(6x(x^2 - 2x - 24)\)

Factor quadratic: \(6x(x - 6)(x + 4)\)

Problem 7

Simplify: \((3x^2y^5)^{-2} \times (2xy^{-3})^3\)

Solution:

\(=(3^{-2}x^{-4}y^{-10}) \times (8x^3y^{-9})\)

\(=8x^{-1}y^{-19} = \frac{8}{xy^{19}}\)

Problem 8

Determine if the relation \{(1,2), (2,4), (3,6), (4,8), (5,10)\} represents a function.

Solution:

Yes, each input has exactly one output. The rule is \(y = 2x\) or \(f(x) = 2x\).

Problem 9

Given \(f(x) = 2x - 5\) and \(g(x) = x^2 + 1\), find \(f(3), f(-2), g(0)\), and \(g(a+1)\).

Solution:

\(f(3) = 2(3) - 5 = 1\)

\(f(-2) = 2(-2) - 5 = -9\)

\(g(0) = 0^2 + 1 = 1\)

\(g(a+1) = (a+1)^2 + 1 = a^2 + 2a + 2\)

Problem 10

Find the slope of the line passing through \( (-2, 5) \) and \( (4, -1) \).

Solution:

\(m = \frac{-1 - 5}{4 - (-2)} = \frac{-6}{6} = -1\)

\(y - 5 = -1(x - (-2)) \Rightarrow y - 5 = -x - 2 \Rightarrow y = -x + 3\)

Problem 11

A taxi charges \($2.50\) for pickup plus \($1.75\) per mile.

Solution:

\(C = 1.75m + 2.50\)

Slope (\(1.75\)): cost per additional mile

Y-intercept (\(2.50\)): base pickup fee

Problem 12

Graph the line \(2x - 3y = 6\) using intercepts.

Solution:

X-intercept (\(y=0\)): \(2x = 6 \Rightarrow x = 3 \Rightarrow (3,0)\)

Y-intercept (\(x=0\)): \(-3y = 6 \Rightarrow y = -2 \Rightarrow (0,-2)\)

Slope-intercept form: \(2x - 3y = 6 \Rightarrow -3y = -2x + 6 \Rightarrow y = \frac{2}{3}x - 2\)

Problem 13

Solve by substitution: \(y = 2x + 1\) and \(3x - y = 4\)

Solution:

\(3x - (2x + 1) = 4 \Rightarrow 3x - 2x - 1 = 4 \Rightarrow x = 5\)

\(y = 2(5) + 1 = 11\)

Solution: \( (5, 11) \)

Problem 14

Solve by elimination: \(2x + 3y = 12\) and \(4x - y = 10\)

Solution:

Multiply second equation by 3: \(12x - 3y = 30\)

Add to first: \(14x = 42 \Rightarrow x = 3\)

Substitute: \(4(3) - y = 10 \Rightarrow y = 2\)

Solution: \( (3, 2) \)

Problem 15

A school sells adult tickets for \($8\) and student tickets for \($5\).

Solution:

Let \(a =\) adult tickets, \(s =\) student tickets

\(a + s = 150\)

\(8a + 5s = 1050\)

From first: \(a = 150 - s\)

Substitute: \(8(150 - s) + 5s = 1050 \Rightarrow 1200 - 8s + 5s = 1050 \Rightarrow -3s = -150 \Rightarrow s = 50\)

\(a = 100\)

100 adult tickets, 50 student tickets
