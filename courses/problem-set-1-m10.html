Problem Set 1: Measurement

Mathematics 10 - Chapter 1 Medium

Problems

Problem 1

Convert \(48\) inches to feet and inches. How many yards is this?

Problem 2

Convert \(1.75\) meters to centimeters and millimeters.

Problem 3

A right pyramid has a square base with side length \(6\) m and slant height \(5\) m. Find its surface area using \(SA = B + \frac{1}{2}Pl\).

Problem 4

A right cone has radius \(4\) cm and slant height \(5\) cm. Find its volume. (Use \(V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h\) and find height first using Pythagorean theorem: \(l^2 = r^2 + h^2\))

Problem 5

A sphere has surface area \(100\pi\) cm². Find its radius and volume using \(SA = 4\pi r^2\) and \(V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3\).

Problem 6

Convert \(2500\) cm to meters and centimeters. Then convert to feet using \(1\) foot \(= 30.48\) cm.

Problem 7

A rectangular prism has dimensions \(8\) ft × \(6\) ft × \(4\) ft. Find its total surface area and volume.

Problem 8

A cylinder has radius \(3\) m and height \(7\) m. Find its surface area \(SA = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi rh\) and volume \(V = \pi r^2 h\). Compare the volume to a cone with the same base and half the height.

Bonus Problems

Bonus Problem 1 Medium

Creative Challenge: A rectangular swimming pool is being filled. The pool's length is twice its width, and its depth is 1.5 m. If the water level rises at a constant rate of 2 cm per minute, how long will it take to fill the pool completely? After 10 minutes, what percentage of the pool's total volume has been filled? Express your answer as a fraction in simplest form.

Bonus Problem 2 Hard

Multi-Shape Puzzle: A solid toy consists of a cube with side length 4 cm, topped by a hemisphere of radius 4 cm (the hemisphere sits on one face of the cube). A cylindrical hole of radius 1 cm is drilled straight through the center of the cube, from top to bottom. Find the total volume of the remaining solid. (Use \(V_{cube} = s^3\), \(V_{hemisphere} = \frac{2}{3}\pi r^3\), \(V_{cylinder} = \pi r^2 h\).)

Bonus Problem 3 Hard

Optimization Mystery: You have 600 cm² of material to make a cylindrical container with a lid. However, the design requires the height to be exactly twice the radius of the base. What are the dimensions of the largest volume container you can make? Find the radius and height, then calculate the maximum volume.
