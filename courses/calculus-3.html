Course Information

  • Level Undergraduate
  • Estimated Duration 14 weeks
  • Prerequisites Calculus II (or equivalent)
  • Recommended Textbooks Calculus: Early Transcendentals (Stewart), Vector Calculus (Marsden)
  • Software Desmos, WolframAlpha, GeoGebra 3D

Course Description

This course covers multivariable calculus including partial derivatives, multiple integrals, and vector calculus. Students will explore functions of several variables, learn techniques for optimization, and study vector fields, line integrals, and surface integrals.

Course Syllabus

  1. Week 1 Functions of Several Variables
  2. Week 2 Partial Derivatives and Gradient
  3. Week 3 Chain Rule and Directional Derivatives
  4. Week 4 Extrema and Lagrange Multipliers
  5. Week 5 Double Integrals and Applications
  6. Week 6 Triple Integrals and Coordinate Systems
  7. Week 7 Vector Fields and Line Integrals
  8. Week 8 Conservative Vector Fields and Green's Theorem
  9. Week 9 Surface Integrals and Stokes' Theorem
  10. Week 10 Divergence Theorem and Applications
  11. Week 11 Parametric Surfaces and Solids
  12. Week 12 Review and Midterm
  13. Week 13 Final Review and Exam Preparation
  14. Week 14 Applications in Physics and Engineering
