Problem Set 10: Permutations, Combinations, Binomial Theorem

Precalculus 12 - Problem Set
Topics: Counting, Probability, Binomial Expansions Medium

Problems

Problem 1

How many different 4-digit PINs can be created using digits 0-9 if repetition is not allowed? How many with repetition allowed?

Problem 2

In how many ways can 5 people be seated around a circular table? How many ways if they sit in a row?

Problem 3

From 8 letters and 4 digits, how many license plates can be made with 2 letters followed by 3 digits? How many with 3 letters followed by 2 digits?

Problem 4

Expand \((2x - 1)^4\) using the binomial theorem. What is the coefficient of \(x^3\)?

Problem 5

A coin is flipped 10 times. What is the probability of getting exactly 6 heads? What is the probability of getting at least 8 heads?

Problem 6

A committee of 4 is chosen from 6 men and 5 women. How many ways if there must be at least 2 women? At most 1 woman?

Problem 7

Find the 5th term in the expansion of \((x + 2)^8\).

Problem 8

A pizza shop offers 8 toppings. How many different 3-topping pizzas are possible? How many total possible pizzas (any number of toppings)?

Bonus Problems

Bonus Problem 1

A committee of 5 is chosen from 7 men and 6 women. In how many ways can this be done if the committee must have at least 2 women and at least 1 man?

Bonus Problem 2

Expand \((x - 1)^7\) using the binomial theorem. Find the sum of all coefficients.
