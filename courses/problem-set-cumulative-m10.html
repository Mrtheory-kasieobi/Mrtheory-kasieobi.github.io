Cumulative Problem Set

Mixed Review - All Topics

Hard

Instructions

This cumulative problem set reviews all topics from Mathematics 10. Work through each section in order, ensuring you understand the concepts before moving on.

Section 1: Measurement

Problem 1:

Convert \(5.2 \text{ meters}\) to centimeters and millimeters. Show your work using dimensional analysis.

Problem 2:

A rectangular prism has dimensions \(8 \text{ cm} \times 5 \text{ cm} \times 3 \text{ cm}\). Calculate its surface area and volume. If the dimensions are doubled, what happens to the surface area and volume?

Section 2: Measurement Applications

Problem 3:

A cylindrical water tank has radius \(r = 2 \text{ m}\) and height \(h = 5 \text{ m}\). Calculate the surface area (including top and bottom) and volume. If water is poured at a rate of \(0.5 \text{ m}^3/\text{min}\), how long to fill the tank?

Section 3: Trigonometry

Problem 4:

In a right triangle, the hypotenuse is \(15 \text{ cm}\) and one angle is \(35°\). Find the lengths of the other two sides. Round to the nearest tenth.

Problem 5:

A ladder leans against a wall, making a \(75°\) angle with the ground. If the foot of the ladder is \(3 \text{ feet}\) from the wall, how long is the ladder?

Section 4: Algebra Prerequisites

Problem 6:

Factor completely: \(6x^3 - 12x^2 - 144x\)

Problem 7:

Simplify: \( (3x^2y^5)^{-2} \times (2xy^{-3})^3 \)

Section 5: Functions and Relations

Problem 8:

Determine if the relation \{(1,2), (2,4), (3,6), (4,8), (5,10)\} represents a function. If it is a function, find the rule.

Problem 9:

Given \(f(x) = 2x - 5\) and \(g(x) = x^2 + 1\), find \(f(3), f(-2), g(0)\), and \(g(a+1)\).

Section 6: Linear Functions

Problem 10:

Find the slope of the line passing through \( (-2, 5) \) and \( (4, -1) \). Write the equation in slope-intercept form.

Problem 11:

A taxi charges \($2.50\) for pickup plus \($1.75\) per mile. Write an equation for the total cost \(C\) for \(m\) miles. What does the slope represent? What does the y-intercept represent?

Problem 12:

Graph the line \(2x - 3y = 6\) using intercepts. Then rewrite in slope-intercept form.

Section 7: Systems of Linear Equations

Problem 13:

Solve by substitution: \(y = 2x + 1\) and \(3x - y = 4\)

Problem 14:

Solve by elimination: \(2x + 3y = 12\) and \(4x - y = 10\)

Problem 15:

A school sells adult tickets for \($8\) and student tickets for \($5\). They sold 150 tickets total for \($1050\). How many of each type were sold?
