Calculus I

Limits, continuity, derivatives, and applications of differential calculus.

Level: Undergraduate Status: In Progress Last Updated: 2026

Course Information

  • Level Undergraduate
  • Estimated Duration 15 weeks
  • Prerequisites Precalculus 12 (or equivalent)
  • Recommended Textbooks Calculus: Early Transcendentals (Stewart)
  • Software Desmos, WolframAlpha

Course Description

This course introduces the fundamental concepts of differential calculus. Students will explore limits, continuity, and the derivative, learning to apply these concepts to solve problems in physics, engineering, and other sciences. The course emphasizes rigorous mathematical reasoning and real-world applications.
