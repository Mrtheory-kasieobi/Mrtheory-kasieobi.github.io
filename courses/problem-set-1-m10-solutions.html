Problem Set 1: Measurement

Mathematics 10 - Chapter 1 Solutions

Solutions

Problem 1: Convert 48 inches to feet and inches. How many yards is this?
There are 12 inches in 1 foot, so 48 inches = 48 ÷ 12 = 4 feet.
Since 4 feet has no remainder, there are 0 inches.
There are 3 feet in 1 yard, so 4 feet = 4 ÷ 3 = \(1\frac{1}{3}\) yards.
Answer: 4 feet 0 inches, or \(1\frac{1}{3}\) yards.
Problem 2: Convert 1.75 meters to centimeters and millimeters.
There are 100 centimeters in 1 meter, so 1.75 m = 1.75 × 100 = 175 cm.
There are 10 millimeters in 1 centimeter, so 175 cm = 175 × 10 = 1750 mm.
Answer: 175 cm and 1750 mm.
Problem 3: A right pyramid has a square base with side length 6 m and slant height 5 m. Find its surface area using SA = B + \(\frac{1}{2}\)Pl.
Base area: B = s² = 6² = 36 m²
Perimeter: P = 4s = 4(6) = 24 m
Lateral area: \(\frac{1}{2}\)Pl = \(\frac{1}{2}\)(24)(5) = 60 m²
Total surface area: SA = B + \(\frac{1}{2}\)Pl = 36 + 60 = 96 m²
Answer: 96 m².
Problem 4: A right cone has radius 4 cm and slant height 5 cm. Find its volume.
First, find the height using Pythagorean theorem: l² = r² + h²
5² = 4² + h²
25 = 16 + h²
h² = 9, so h = 3 cm
Volume: V = \(\frac{1}{3}\)\(\pi r^2 h\) = \(\frac{1}{3}\)\(\pi (4)^2(3)\) = \(\frac{1}{3}\)\(\pi (16)(3)\) = 16\(\pi\) cm³
Answer: 16\(\pi\) cm³ (or approximately 50.27 cm³).
Problem 5: A sphere has surface area 100\(\pi\) cm². Find its radius and volume.
Surface area: SA = 4\(\pi\)r² = 100\(\pi\)
4r² = 100, so r² = 25, thus r = 5 cm
Volume: V = \(\frac{2}{3}\)\(\pi r^3\) = \(\frac{2}{3}\)\(\pi (5)^3\) = \(\frac{2}{3}\)\(\pi (125)\) = 125\(\frac{2}{3}\)\(\pi\) cm³
Answer: Radius = 5 cm, Volume = 125\(\frac{2}{3}\)\(\pi\) cm³.
Problem 6: Convert 2500 cm to meters and centimeters. Then convert to feet.
2500 cm = 2500 ÷ 100 = 25 m
25 m = 2500 cm (already given)
Convert to feet: 2500 cm × (1 ft / 30.48 cm) = 82.02 ft
Answer: 25 m and 2500 cm, which is approximately 82.02 feet.
Problem 7: A rectangular prism has dimensions 8 ft × 6 ft × 4 ft. Find its total surface area and volume.
Volume: V = l × w × h = 8 × 6 × 4 = 192 ft³
Surface area: SA = 2(lw + lh + wh) = 2[(8)(6) + (8)(4) + (6)(4)]
SA = 2[48 + 32 + 24] = 2(104) = 208 ft²
Answer: Volume = 192 ft³, Surface Area = 208 ft².
Problem 8: A cylinder has radius 3 m and height 7 m. Find its surface area and volume. Compare to a cone with same base and half the height.
Cylinder surface area: SA = \(2\pi r^2 + 2\pi rh\) = \(2\pi(3)^2 + 2\pi(3)(7)\)
SA = \(2\pi(9) + 42\pi = 18\pi + 42\pi = 60\pi\) m²
Cylinder volume: V = \(\pi r^2 h\) = \(\pi(3)^2(7)\) = 63\(\pi\) m³
Cone: same base radius r = 3 m, height h = 7/2 = 3.5 m
Cone volume: V = \(\frac{1}{3}\)\(\pi r^2 h\) = \(\frac{1}{3}\)\(\pi(3)^2(3.5)\) = \(\frac{1}{3}\)\(\pi(9)(3.5)\) = 10.5\(\pi\) m³
Cone SA = \(\pi r^2 + \pi rl\), where \(l = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2}\) = \(\sqrt{9 + 12.25}\) = \(\sqrt{21.25}\) ≈ 4.61 m
Cone SA ≈ \(\pi(9) + \pi(3)(4.61)\) ≈ 28.27 + 43.45 ≈ 71.72 m²
Answer: Cylinder V = 63\(\pi\) m³, Cone V = 10.5\(\pi\) m³. Cylinder has larger volume.

Bonus Solutions

Bonus Problem 1: Swimming pool filling rate.
Pool dimensions: length = 2w, width = w, depth = 1.5 m
Volume = length × width × depth = 2w × w × 1.5 = 3w² m³
Water rise rate = 2 cm/min = 0.02 m/min
Cross-sectional area (width × depth) = w × 1.5 = 1.5w m²
Time to fill: total height 1.5 m ÷ rate 0.02 m/min = 75 minutes
Volume filled in 10 min: 1.5w × 0.02 × 10 = 3w m³
Percentage: (3w)/(3w²) = 1/w... wait, need actual dimensions
Let w = 2 m (assumed), then length = 4 m, volume = 12 m³
In 10 min: 4 × 2 × 0.2 = 1.6 m³ filled
Percentage: 1.6/12 = 2/15
Answer: 75 minutes to fill; 2/15 filled after 10 minutes.
Bonus Problem 2: Multi-shape puzzle.
Cube volume: V = 4³ = 64 cm³
Hemisphere volume: V = \(\frac{128}{3}\)\(\pi\) cm³
Cylinder volume: V = \(\pi(1)^2(4)\) = 4\(\pi\) cm³
Total volume = cube + hemisphere - cylinder
V = 64 + \(\frac{128}{3}\)\(\pi\) - 4\(\pi\) = 64 + \(\frac{116}{3}\)\(\pi\) cm³
Answer: 64 + \(\frac{116}{3}\)\(\pi\) cm³ ≈ 166.2 cm³.
Bonus Problem 3: Optimization mystery.
Constraint: \(h = 2r\), Surface area \(= 600 \text{ cm}^2\)
For a cylinder: \(SA = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi rh\)
Substitute \(h = 2r\):
\(SA = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi r(2r) = 2\pi r^2 + 4\pi r^2 = 6\pi r^2\)
Set \(SA = 600\):
\(6\pi r^2 = 600\)
\(r^2 = \frac{100}{\pi}\)
\(r = \frac{10}{\sqrt{\pi}} \approx 5.64 \text{ cm}\)
\(h = 2r = \frac{20}{\sqrt{\pi}} \approx 11.28 \text{ cm}\)
Volume: \(V = \pi r^2 h\)
\(V = \pi \cdot \frac{100}{\pi} \cdot \frac{20}{\sqrt{\pi}} = \frac{2000}{\sqrt{\pi}} \approx 1128.4 \text{ cm}^3\)
Answer: \(r = \frac{10}{\sqrt{\pi}}\) cm, \(h = \frac{20}{\sqrt{\pi}}\) cm, \(V = \frac{2000}{\sqrt{\pi}}\) cm³.
