Function Operations

Precalculus 12 - Lecture Notes
British Columbia Curriculum | Last Updated: 2026

1. Sums and Differences of Functions

Given two functions \(f(x)\) and \(g(x)\), we can create new functions by adding or subtracting them.

Sum and Difference:
\[(f + g)(x) = f(x) + g(x)\]
\[(f - g)(x) = f(x) - g(x)\]

1.1 Domain of Sum/Difference

The domain of \((f + g)(x)\) or \((f - g)(x)\) is the intersection of the domains of \(f(x)\) and \(g(x)\).

Example 1: Sum of Functions

Given \(f(x) = x^2\) and \(g(x) = \sqrt{x}\), find \((f + g)(x)\) and its domain.

\((f + g)(x) = x^2 + \sqrt{x}\)

Domain: \([0, \infty)\) (intersection of \(\mathbb{R}\) and \([0, \infty)\))

2. Products and Quotients of Functions

2.1 Product of Functions

\[(f \cdot g)(x) = f(x) \cdot g(x)\]

2.2 Quotient of Functions

\[(f \div g)(x) = \frac{f(x)}{g(x)}, \quad g(x) \neq 0\]
Example 2: Product and Quotient

Given \(f(x) = x + 1\) and \(g(x) = x - 1\), find \((f \cdot g)(x)\) and \((f \div g)(x)\).

\((f \cdot g)(x) = (x + 1)(x - 1) = x^2 - 1\)

\((f \div g)(x) = \frac{x + 1}{x - 1}\), domain: \(x \neq 1\)

3. Composite Functions

3.1 Definition

A composite function is formed by applying one function to the result of another.

Composite Function: \((f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x))\)

3.2 Domain of Composite Functions

The domain of \((f \circ g)(x)\) consists of all \(x\) in the domain of \(g\) such that \(g(x)\) is in the domain of \(f\).

Example 3: Composite Functions

Given \(f(x) = x^2\) and \(g(x) = x + 1\), find \((f \circ g)(x)\) and \((g \circ f)(x)\).

\((f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x)) = f(x + 1) = (x + 1)^2 = x^2 + 2x + 1\)

\((g \circ f)(x) = g(f(x)) = g(x^2) = x^2 + 1\)

Note: \((f \circ g)(x) \neq (g \circ f)(x)\)

3.3 Decomposing Functions

We can decompose a function into simpler functions to understand its structure.

Example 4: Decomposing Functions

Write \(h(x) = \sqrt{x^2 + 1}\) as a composition of two functions.

Let \(g(x) = x^2 + 1\) and \(f(x) = \sqrt{x}\)

Then \(h(x) = (f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x))\)

4. Practice Problems

Problem 1

Given \(f(x) = 2x - 1\) and \(g(x) = x^2 + 3\), find \((f - g)(x)\) and its domain.

Problem 2

Find \((f \circ g)(x)\) for \(f(x) = \frac{1}{x}\) and \(g(x) = x - 2\).

Problem 3

Decompose \(h(x) = \sin(x^2 + 1)\) into two functions.
