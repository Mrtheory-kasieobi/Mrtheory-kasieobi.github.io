Problem Set 9: Systems of Equations

Precalculus 11 · 8 problems Medium

Problems

Problem 1

Solve by substitution: \(\begin{cases} y = 2x - 3 \\ 3x + y = 7 \end{cases}\)

Problem 2

Solve by elimination: \(\begin{cases} 2x + 3y = 8 \\ 4x - y = 6 \end{cases}\)

Problem 3

Solve by substitution: \(\begin{cases} x^2 + y^2 = 25 \\ y = x + 1 \end{cases}\)

Problem 4

Solve the system: \(\begin{cases} 2x - y = 4 \\ 3x + 2y = 11 \end{cases}\)

Problem 5

A movie theater sold 200 tickets. Adult tickets cost $12, child tickets cost $8. Total revenue was $2160. Find the number of each type of ticket.

Problem 6

Solve: \(\begin{cases} 3x - 2y = 7 \\ x + 4y = 1 \end{cases}\)

Problem 7

Find the intersection point of \(y = 2x + 3\) and \(y = -x + 9\).

Problem 8

Solve: \(\begin{cases} y = x^2 - 4 \\ y = 2x + 1 \end{cases}\)

Bonus Problems

Bonus Problem 1 Hard

Solve: \(\begin{cases} 2x + 3y - z = 1 \\ x - y + 2z = 4 \\ 3x + y + z = 7 \end{cases}\). Then solve \(\begin{cases} a + 2b = 3 \\ 2a - b + c = 1 \\ a + b - c = 2 \end{cases}\)

Bonus Problem 2 Hard

Find all points of intersection between \(y = x^2 - 2x - 3\) and \(y = -\frac{1}{2}x + 4\). Then find where these curves are closest.
