Course Description

This course is the continuation of Algebra I, following MIT's 18.702 structure. Topics include group representations, rings and ideals, fields, polynomial rings, modules, factorization, integers in quadratic number fields, field extensions, and Galois theory. The course continues the rigorous proof-based approach to abstract algebra, building on the foundational concepts from Algebra I.

Course Syllabus

  1. Week 1 Review of Algebra I
  2. Week 2 Group Representations
  3. Week 3 Rings and Ideals
  4. Week 4 Maximal and Prime Ideals
  5. Week 5 Quotient Rings and Isomorphism Theorems

  7. Week 7 Unique Factorization in Polynomial Rings
  8. Week 8 Modules over Principal Ideal Domains
  9. Week 9 Field Extensions and Algebraic Elements
  10. Week 10 Splitting Fields and Normal Extensions
  11. Week 11 Galois Theory
  12. Week 12 Galois Correspondence and Applications
  13. Week 13 Quadratic Field Extensions
  14. Week 14 Final Review and Exam Preparation
