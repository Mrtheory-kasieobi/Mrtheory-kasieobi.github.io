Introduction to Proofs

Logic, set theory, and fundamental techniques of mathematical proof.

Level: Undergraduate Status: In Progress Last Updated: 2026

Course Information

  • Level Undergraduate
  • Estimated Duration 12 weeks
  • Prerequisites Calculus I (recommended)
  • Recommended Textbooks How to Prove It (Velleman)
  • Software None required

Course Description

This course introduces the foundational language and methods of mathematical proof. Students will learn propositional and predicate logic, set theory, and the principal proof techniques used in advanced mathematics: direct proof, proof by contradiction, contrapositive, induction, and combinatorial arguments. The course develops the rigorous thinking essential for all higher mathematics.

Course Syllabus

  1. Week 1 Propositional Logic and Truth Tables
  2. Week 2 Predicate Logic and Quantifiers
  3. Week 3 Set Theory: Operations and Relations
  4. Week 4 Functions and Relations
  5. Week 5 Direct Proof and Proof by Contrapositive
  6. Week 6 Proof by Contradiction
  7. Week 7 Mathematical Induction
  8. Week 8 Strong Induction and Well-Ordering
  9. Week 9 Divisibility and Modular Arithmetic
  10. Week 10 Combinatorial Proofs
  11. Week 11 Infinite Sets and Cardinality
  12. Week 12 Review and Proof Writing Workshop
