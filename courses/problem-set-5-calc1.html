Problem Set 5: Trig Derivatives and Optimization

Calculus I - Week 5

Instructions

Show all work. No calculators. Write clearly and show each step.

Problems

  • 1. Find \(\frac{dy}{dx}\) for \(y = \sin^3(2x)\)
  • 2. Find \(\frac{d}{dx}[\tan^{-1}(x^3)]\)
  • 3. Find the critical points of \(f(x) = x + 2\sin x\) on \([0, 2\pi]\)
  • 4. Find the dimensions of the rectangle with perimeter 20 having maximum area
  • 5. A rectangular box with square base is to have volume 1000 cm³. Find dimensions for minimum surface area
  • 6. Find \(\frac{dy}{dx}\) for \(y = \sec x \cdot \cot x\)
  • 7. Find the local maximum and minimum values of \(f(x) = \frac{x^2}{x^2 + 1}\)
  • 8. A ladder 15 ft long rests against a wall. If the bottom slides toward the wall at 3 ft/s, how fast is the top moving when the bottom is 9 ft from the wall?
  • 9. Find \(\frac{d}{dx}[\sin^{-1}(x) + \cos^{-1}(x)]\)
  • 10. Prove that among all rectangles with fixed perimeter, the square has maximum area
Bonus Problems (10 points each):
  • B1. Find the absolute maximum and minimum of \(f(x) = x^3 - 12x\) on \([-3, 3]\)
  • B2. A cylindrical can is to hold 1 liter of oil. Find the dimensions that minimize the cost of metal for the can
