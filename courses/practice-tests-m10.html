Practice Tests

Mathematics 10 · Two full-length assessments

Each practice test contains 20 questions.

Part A is multiple choice — select the best answer for each question.

Part B is written response — show all of your work. Some questions have multiple parts.

Use these tests to check your understanding of measurement, trigonometry, polynomials, exponents and radicals, relations and functions, linear functions, and systems of equations.

Practice Test 1

10 multiple choice · 10 written response

Practice Test 2

10 multiple choice · 10 written response

Practice Test 1

Part A: Multiple Choice

Choose the best answer for each question.

1.

Convert \(5\) feet to inches.

2.

What is the surface area of a cube with side length \(3\) cm?

3.

In a right triangle, which ratio equals \(\tan\theta\)?

4.

Solve for \(x\): \(2x + 5 = 17\).

5.

Factor \(x^2 + 5x + 6\).

6.

Simplify \((x^3)(x^4)\).

7.

What is \(\sqrt{64}\)?

8.

Find the slope of the line through \((1,2)\) and \((3,8)\).

9.

Solve the system: \(x + y = 5\), \(x - y = 1\).

10.

What is the domain of \(f(x) = \dfrac{1}{x - 2}\)?

Part B: Written Response

Show all of your work.

11.

Convert \(2.4\) m to centimetres, then to millimetres. Then convert \(2.4\) m to feet using \(1\text{ ft} = 30.48\text{ cm}\). Round your answer to two decimal places.

12.

A right cone has radius \(3\) cm and height \(4\) cm.

  1. Find the slant height \(l\) using \(l^2 = r^2 + h^2\).
  2. Find the volume using \(V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h\).
  3. Find the total surface area using \(SA = \pi r^2 + \pi r l\).
13.

In a right triangle, the hypotenuse is \(10\) and one angle is \(30^\circ\).

  1. Find the length of the side opposite the \(30^\circ\) angle.
  2. Find the length of the side adjacent to the \(30^\circ\) angle. Leave exact answers (you may use \(\sqrt{3}\)).
14.

Complete each algebra task.

  1. Expand and simplify \((x+3)(x-2)\).
  2. Factor \(x^2 - 9\).
  3. Simplify \((2x^2)(3x^3)\).
15.

Work with exponent laws.

  1. State the law for \(\dfrac{x^a}{x^b}\).
  2. Simplify \(\dfrac{x^5}{x^2}\).
  3. Evaluate \(2^3 \cdot 2^2\).
16.

A line passes through \((0,3)\) and has slope \(2\).

  1. Write the equation in slope-intercept form.
  2. Rewrite the equation in standard form \(Ax + By = C\).
17.

Solve the system by substitution:

\(y = 2x\)
\(x + y = 9\)

18.

Solve the system by elimination:

\(2x + y = 10\)
\(x - y = 2\)

19.

A rectangular prism has dimensions \(5\) cm \(\times\) \(4\) cm \(\times\) \(3\) cm.

  1. Find its total surface area.
  2. Find its volume.
20.

Given \(f(x) = 2x - 1\):

  1. Find \(f(3)\).
  2. Find \(x\) when \(f(x) = 7\).
  3. State the domain and range of \(f\).

Practice Test 2

Part A: Multiple Choice

Choose the best answer for each question.

1.

Convert \(36\) inches to yards. (\(1\) yard \(= 3\) feet \(= 36\) inches)

2.

What is the volume of a sphere with radius \(3\)? (\(V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3\))

3.

In a right triangle, the opposite side is \(5\), the adjacent side is \(12\), and the hypotenuse is \(13\). What is \(\cos\theta\)?

4.

Solve for \(x\): \(3(x - 2) = 12\).

5.

Factor \(x^2 - 4x + 4\).

6.

Simplify \((x^2)^3\).

7.

What is \(\sqrt[3]{27}\)?

8.

What is the \(y\)-intercept of \(y = -2x + 5\)?

9.

Solve for \(x\): \(2x + 3 = 11\).

10.

What is the range of \(f(x) = x^2\)?

Part B: Written Response

Show all of your work.

11.

Convert \(150\) cm to metres, then to inches. (\(1\text{ in} = 2.54\text{ cm}\)). Round to two decimal places.

12.

A cylinder has radius \(2\) m and height \(5\) m.

  1. Find the volume using \(V = \pi r^2 h\).
  2. Find the total surface area using \(SA = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi r h\).
13.

In an isosceles right triangle, one leg has length \(7\).

  1. Find the length of the other leg.
  2. Find the length of the hypotenuse. Leave the exact answer.
14.

Complete each algebra task.

  1. Expand and simplify \((2x+1)(x-3)\).
  2. Factor \(2x^2 + 5x + 3\).
  3. Simplify \(\dfrac{4x^3}{2x}\).
15.

Work with exponent laws.

  1. Simplify \((x^4)^2\).
  2. Evaluate \(3^4\).
  3. Simplify \((a^2b^3)(a^3b)\).
16.

A line passes through \((2,1)\) and \((2,7)\).

  1. Describe the line (is it vertical, horizontal, or neither)?
  2. Write its equation and state whether the slope is defined.
17.

Solve the system:

\(x + 2y = 8\)
\(3x - 2y = 4\)

18.

Solve the system:

\(5x - 2y = 1\)
\(x + y = 5\)

19.

A sphere has radius \(6\) cm.

  1. Find its surface area using \(SA = 4\pi r^2\).
  2. Find its volume using \(V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3\).
20.

Given \(f(x) = x^2 - 4\):

  1. Find \(f(2)\).
  2. Find the \(x\)-intercepts.
  3. State the vertex and the equation of the axis of symmetry.
