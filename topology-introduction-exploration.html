Topology: A Curious Discovery

July 13, 2026 ~3 min read Learning Journal

So I got curious about topology and decided to look at the basic definitions. It's fascinating how topology studies properties that remain unchanged under continuous deformation. The idea that a coffee cup and a donut are "the same" in topology was mind-blowing to me.

What I Learned

The basic definitions of a topology involve open sets and their axioms. What's interesting is how this abstract framework captures the essence of "closeness" without needing a notion of distance. It's a completely different way of thinking about space and continuity.

Key Definitions

Topological Space: A set X together with a collection T of subsets of X (called open sets) satisfying:

  • The empty set ∅ and X are in T
  • Any union of elements in T is in T
  • Any finite intersection of elements in T is in T

Closed Set: The complement of an open set.

Continuous Function: A function f: X → Y between topological spaces is continuous if the preimage of every open set in Y is open in X.

Homeomorphism: A continuous bijection with a continuous inverse. This is what makes a coffee cup and a donut "the same" in topology.

My Current Situation

After learning the definitions, I realized I'm not mathematically ready yet. I still need to solidify my understanding of set theory, real analysis, and abstract algebra first. Topology seems like it will become clearer once I've built up those foundations.

Future Plans

I've decided that I will start to learn Topology in the future, once I'm done learning some other prerequisites. The subject has really interested me, and I can't wait to dive deeper once I'm better prepared.

"Topology is not a subject, it is a way of thinking." – Unknown

Right now, I'm focusing on other areas, but topology will definitely be on my learning roadmap. The beauty of it is that it connects so many different branches of mathematics.
