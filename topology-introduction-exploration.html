Exploring Topology: My Journey into a New Mathematical World

July 13, 2026 ~3 min read

I've always been fascinated by "shape" and "space" in mathematics, but it wasn't until I stumbled upon topology that I truly understood what it means to study continuity, connectedness, and the properties of spaces that stay the same even when bent or stretched.

Why Topology?

The idea that a coffee cup and a donut are "the same" in topology was mind-blowing. It's not about exact measurements or angles—it's about properties that remain unchanged under continuous deformation. This abstraction felt both surprising and powerful.

My Learning Path

I'm currently working through the basics:

  • Topological spaces: Understanding open sets and their axioms
  • Continuous functions: How they preserve "closeness"
  • Homeomorphisms: The formal way we say two spaces are "the same"
  • Compactness: A generalization of closed and bounded sets
  • Connectedness: When a space can't be split into two pieces

Where I'm Going

Once I master these fundamentals, I want to explore:

  • Algebraic topology (using algebra to study topological spaces)
  • Point-set topology (the rigorous foundations)
  • Applications to other areas of mathematics
"Topology is not a subject, it is a way of thinking." – Unknown

For now, I'm taking it one concept at a time. The beauty of topology is that it connects to so many areas—analysis, algebra, geometry—and that's what keeps me coming back to my notes.
