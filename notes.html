Welcome to my collection of mathematical notes. This section is a record of my ongoing study and exploration of mathematics, covering material ranging from olympiad problem solving to undergraduate and graduate-level mathematics. The notes are intended to help me organize what I learn while developing a deeper understanding of the definitions, techniques, proofs, examples, and connections that arise throughout different areas of mathematics. These notes are different from the more polished and self-contained material in my Expository Writing section. Some may focus on developing the theory of a subject from its foundations, while others may collect results, work through examples and proofs, or explore connections between different areas. At the moment, this section is still under development, and I do not yet have any notes that I would consider fully complete. New notes will be added and existing ones will be expanded, revised, and refined as my understanding develops. The notes are organized into three broad categories: Olympiad Mathematics, Undergraduate Mathematics, and Graduate Mathematics.
