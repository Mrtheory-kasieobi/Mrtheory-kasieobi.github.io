About Me

Hi! As you may have already seen on the home page, I'm Kasieobi (Kasi) Udumaga, a high school student passionate about mathematics and understanding it at a deep level. I've been interested in mathematics since I was 9 years old, although mathematics wasn't always my main interest. I originally found physics more interesting than mathematics, and it wasn't until the end of Grade 7 that my interests shifted more strongly toward mathematics. Since discovering proof-based math, I've been drawn to how simple definitions can lead to complex theories. Rather than memorizing techniques, I focus on asking why things are true.

My current interests include commutative algebra and algebraic number theory. I hope to contribute original research in these areas. For now, I spend my time reading textbooks, writing notes, solving challenging problems (sometimes), and developing the mathematical maturity and tools needed for research.

This website essentially documents that journey. It's a place where I organize notes, share expository writing, document projects, and explore ideas I find interesting. More than anything, I hope it becomes a record of my growth as a mathematician from what I'm learning now to the research I'll pursue in the future.

Outside of mathematics, I'm Kasi. I enjoy sprinting and compete in track and field, where I've learned discipline, perseverance, and consistency. I also spend a lot of time playing Geometry Dash, where I enjoy progressing through the game and grinding stars. As of writing, I have 25,344 stars. I also enjoy playing GeoGuessr, especially because it lets me explore different places, structures, landscapes, and cultures around the world. One of my favourite parts is learning the different metas and figuring out how small clues can reveal where a location is. Beyond that, I enjoy anime, J-pop, and K-pop. My favourite song is "Hyouriittai" by YUZU. These interests are quite different from mathematics, but they are all things I enjoy spending time on outside of studying.
