Research Portfolio

Research in algebraic number theory, and commutative algebra.

Primary Mathematical Interests

The core areas forming the foundation of my mathematical development and research direction.

Commutative Algebra

I am interested in the study of ideals, modules, rings, and their structural properties, particularly questions involving dimension, factorization, and localization. My interests are still developing, and I am exploring different areas of commutative algebra to identify more specific research directions.

Prime Ideals Integral Dependence Krull Domains Dedekind Domains Krull dimension

Algebraic Number Theory

I am currently exploring Algebraic Number Theory and its approach to understanding arithmetic through algebraic structures. My interests include number fields, ideals, valuations, factorization, and the behavior of primes, and I am continuing to investigate which aspects of the subject I would ultimately like to pursue more deeply.

Number Fields Ideal Class Groups Prime Ideal Factorization Ramification Theory Transcendence Theory

Secondary Mathematical Interests

Areas that complement my primary interests and represent substantial potential for exploration.

Arithmetic Geometry

I am exploring Arithmetic Geometry and the interaction between algebraic geometry and number theory. I am interested in how geometric methods can be used to study arithmetic and Diophantine questions, but my interests in this area are still developing as I learn more about the subject and its connections with other areas of mathematics.

Diophantine Geometry Algebraic curves Elliptic curves Rational points

Transcendence Theory

I am currently exploring Transcendence Theory, with a particular interest in the arithmetic properties of transcendental numbers and their connections to Algebraic Number Theory. I am interested in transcendence theorems, algebraic independence, and Diophantine applications, including the Gelfond–Schneider and Lindemann–Weierstrass theorems.

Transcendental Numbers Transcendence Algebraic Independence Gelfond–Schneider Lindemann–Weierstrass Diophantine Equations

Algebraic Geometry

I am interested in Algebraic Geometry and its connections to Commutative Algebra and Number Theory. I would like to explore how algebraic structures can be used to study geometric objects, particularly varieties, algebraic curves, and related Diophantine questions.

Algebraic Varieties Algebraic Curves Schemes Commutative Algebra Number Theory

Future Research Interests

Areas I plan to explore as my mathematical foundation deepens.

Noncommutative ring theory

I am interested in Noncommutative Ring Theory and the ways in which familiar ideas from Commutative Algebra change when multiplication is not commutative. I am interested in the structure of noncommutative rings, ideals, and modules.

Noncommutative Rings Ring Theory Ideals Modules

Geometric measure theory

I am interested in Geometric Measure Theory and its approach to studying geometric objects using ideas from measure theory and analysis. I would like to explore topics such as Hausdorff measure and dimension, rectifiability, and the study of geometric structures through analytic methods.

Hausdorff Measure Hausdorff Dimension Rectifiability Geometric Analysis Minimal Surfaces
