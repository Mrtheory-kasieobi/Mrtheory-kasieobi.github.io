Research & Mathematical Interests

This page outlines my current mathematical interests and the areas I am exploring as I develop toward research. It distinguishes between primary areas of focus, secondary interests, and future directions, while also providing a place for eventual research notes and working investigations.

Primary Mathematical Interests

Commutative Algebra

I am interested in the structural theory of commutative rings and modules, particularly ideals, localization, integral extensions, dimension theory, and factorization. I am especially interested in the relationships between these algebraic structures and their arithmetic and geometric interpretations. Of particular interest is the role of commutative algebra in Algebraic Number Theory, including the study of Dedekind domains, integral closures, prime ideals, and the structural properties of rings arising from number fields.

Algebraic Number Theory

My primary mathematical interest is Algebraic Number Theory, particularly the structural and arithmetic properties of number fields and their extensions. I am interested in algebraic integers, ideals, Dedekind domains, valuations, prime decomposition, class groups, units, and Galois theory. Beyond the classical theory, I am interested in class field theory, local fields, p-adic methods, and Galois representations, particularly their roles in understanding deeper arithmetic structures. More broadly, I am interested in structural problems in modern number theory and the connections between algebraic structures, field extensions, and Diophantine equations.

Secondary Mathematical Interests

Arithmetic Geometry

I am interested in Arithmetic Geometry, particularly the interaction between algebraic geometry and number theory in the study of Diophantine problems. My interests include rational and integral points on algebraic varieties, elliptic curves, abelian varieties, and the arithmetic properties of algebraic curves. I am also interested in how geometric structures and arithmetic invariants can be used to understand the existence and distribution of solutions to Diophantine equations.

Algebraic Geometry

I am interested in Algebraic Geometry, particularly the algebraic and geometric structures underlying varieties and schemes. My interests include affine and projective varieties, morphisms, divisors, coordinate rings, and the relationship between geometric objects and their corresponding algebraic structures. I am particularly interested in its connections to Commutative Algebra and Arithmetic Geometry, including how geometric methods extend to the study of arithmetic objects and Diophantine questions.

Iwasawa Theory

I am interested in Iwasawa Theory and its study of arithmetic phenomena across infinite extensions of number fields, particularly cyclotomic extensions and their associated Galois groups. My interests include class groups in towers of number fields, Iwasawa modules, p-adic L-functions, and the arithmetic information encoded by their algebraic structures. I am particularly interested in the connections between Iwasawa Theory, class field theory, Galois representations, and broader questions concerning the arithmetic of number fields.

Future Research Interests

Noncommutative Ring Theory

I am interested in Noncommutative Ring Theory, particularly the structural properties of rings, ideals, and modules when multiplication is not commutative. My interests include noncommutative extensions of familiar algebraic constructions, representation theory, and the relationships between ring-theoretic structures and their associated algebraic invariants. I would also like to explore potential connections between noncommutative algebra, representation theory, and modern number theory.
