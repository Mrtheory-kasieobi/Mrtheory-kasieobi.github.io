Primary Mathematical Interests

My mathematical exploration centers on algebraic number theory, arithmetic geometry, algebraic geometry, and commutative algebra.

Commutative Algebra

I am interested in the study of ideals, modules, rings, and their structural properties, particularly questions involving dimension, factorization, and localization. My interests are still developing, and I am exploring different areas of commutative algebra to identify more specific research directions.

Prime Ideals Integral Dependence Krull Domains Dedekind Domains Krull dimension

Arithmetic Geometry

I am exploring Arithmetic Geometry and the interaction between algebraic geometry and number theory. I am interested in how geometric methods can be used to study arithmetic and Diophantine questions, but my interests in this area are still developing as I learn more about the subject and its connections with other areas of mathematics.

Diophantine Geometry Algebraic curves Elliptic curves Rational points Commutative algebra Algebraic number theory

Algebraic Number Theory

I am currently exploring Algebraic Number Theory and its approach to understanding arithmetic through algebraic structures. My interests include number fields, ideals, valuations, factorization, and the behavior of primes, and I am continuing to investigate which aspects of the subject I would ultimately like to pursue more deeply.

Number Fields Ideal Class Groups Prime Ideal Factorization Ramification Theory Transcendence Theory
