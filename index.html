Welcome

Hello! I'm Kasieobi Udumaga, a high school student from British Columbia, Canada with an interest in pure mathematics.

My primary interests include algebraic number theory and commutative algebra. This website documents my learning, research projects, expository writing, and mathematical notes.

Outside Mathematics, I am a sprinter and enjoy track and field. I am a 13 time provincial champion in the 100m and 200m. I am also a co-holder of the electronically timed provincial record for the U15 100m.

Major Website updates are typically released every 4-6 weeks, though the schedule may vary depending on the time of the year.
