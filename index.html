Welcome

I am a high school student in British Columbia, Canada, interested primarily in pure mathematics, particularly algebraic number theory, arithmetic geometry, and commutative algebra.

My mathematical interests extend across number theory, abstract algebra, analysis, and geometry. I am especially interested in the arithmetic of number fields, Diophantine equations, Galois representations, and the structures underlying modern number theory.

This website primarily serves as an archive of my mathematical development, containing independent mathematical investigations, expository writing, research projects, mathematical notes, and explorations ranging from olympiad mathematics to graduate mathematics.

Outside of mathematics, I am a sprinter and enjoy track and field. I am a 12-time provincial champion in the 100m and 200m and a co-holder of the electronically timed provincial record for the U15 100m dash. I obtained a silver medal at legion nationals in the 100m with a time of 11.20(0.0) in 2026.
