Welcome

Hello! I'm Kasieobi Udumaga, a high school student from British Columbia, Canada with a strong interest in pure mathematics.

My primary interests include algebra, algebraic number theory, commutative algebra, and arithmetic geometry. This website documents my learning, research projects, expository writing, and mathematical notes.

Outside Mathematics, I am a sprinter and enjoy track and field.  I am a 13 time provincial champion in the 100m and 200m. I am also the tied electronically timed provincial record holder in the U15 100m.
