Track & Field

Competitive Journey

I currently compete for the Prince George Track & Field Club and have represented British Columbia at national competitions (Just one legion nationals lol). Over the years, I have had the opportunity to race against many of Canada's fastest young sprinters, experiences that continue to challenge me to improve both physically and mentally.

The Mathematical Mindset

Although mathematics and sprinting may seem unrelated, I have found that they complement each other remarkably well. Mathematics teaches logical reasoning, creativity, and patience when solving difficult problems. Sprinting develops resilience, confidence, and the ability to perform under pressure. Both require sustained dedication over many years, and both reward careful preparation far more than natural talent alone.

Progress and Growth

One of the aspects I enjoy most about sprinting is that progress is measurable. Every hundredth of a second represents months of work, and every race provides immediate feedback on what can be improved.

Future Goals

As I continue my academic journey into commutative algebra and algebraic number theory, I also hope to continue competing at a high level in track and field. Whether I am preparing for a race or working through a difficult proof, my goal remains the same: to become a little better each day.
